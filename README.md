## Key Features

**Multi-Model Food Detection**

* Utilizes three AI models to analyze uploaded food images
* Enhances accuracy through model comparison

**Nutritional Analysis**

* Provides a detailed breakdown of calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fats
* Offers complete nutritional composition of food

**Health Alert System**

* Identifies risky foods (e.g., high sugar for diabetic users)
* Generates real-time warning messages

**Recommended Portion Size**

* Suggests optimal quantity for consumption
* Supports maintaining a balanced diet

**Health Benefits of Food**

* Highlights nutritional advantages of selected food
* Promotes awareness of healthy eating habits

**Healthier Alternatives**

* Recommends better food options
* Includes direct purchase links (Swiggy, Zomato, etc.)

**NutriBot AI Assistant**

* AI-powered chatbot for personalized nutrition guidance
* Answers user queries related to diet and health
